Overview
Executive Summary

Why now, and why Fabulaa is perfectly positioned to disrupt the $8B global AAC market.

🚨

The Problem

Premium AAC apps like Proloquo2Go ($249.99), Tobii Dynavox ($25/mo+), and CoughDrop ($15/mo) price out millions of families — especially in developing regions — who need them the most.

💡

The Opportunity

Fabulaa already has the right mission — free & accessible. Version 2.0 will be a full-stack, AI-powered AAC platform that matches or exceeds premium competitors while staying free or affordable.

🚀

The Model

Free core app for all users + optional Fabulaa Pro subscription for advanced AI features + B2B partnerships with schools and clinics for sustainable long-term revenue.

Platform Strategy
Recommended Architecture

One unified platform with multiple user modes — the same approach that makes Google Maps serve walkers, drivers, and cyclists perfectly in a single app.

💡 Strategic Recommendation: One Unified App, Not Multiple

Users with disabilities benefit enormously from consistency. One app with layered modes maximises user retention, simplifies maintenance, and drives stronger App Store ratings — outperforming fragmented multi-app strategies every time.

Feature Plan
20 New Features to Build

Organised into three tiers: Free core features, advanced Free+Pro, and AI-powered differentiators that no competitor offers today.

🔵 TIER 1 — Core Features (Free)

🧩

Robust AAC Symbol Board

10,000+ free ARASAAC symbols, core word vocabulary, flexible 2×2 to 10×10 grids, sentence strip builder with motor-planning consistent layouts.

FREE 🔥 Critical
🔊

High-Quality TTS Voices

Natural voices via Google TTS / Azure. 8 languages at launch expanding to 20+. Male, female, child voice options. Rate & pitch control. Custom recorded voice support.

FREE
🖼️

Visual Scene Display

Photo-based communication boards — tap regions of real-world photos to speak. Users create their own scenes with personal photos. Proven highly effective for children.

FREE
💬

Smart Phrase & Quick Chat

Upgraded phrase library with time-of-day context awareness, one-tap most-used phrases, and an always-accessible emergency phrase strip that cannot be hidden.

FREE
✏️

Enhanced AI Scribble

Draw → AI image recognition suggests matching AAC symbol. Handwriting-to-text conversion for users who can write. Share drawings with caregivers instantly.

FREE
📵

Full Offline Mode

100% offline functionality for core communication. All symbols, phrases, and voices cached locally. Syncs automatically when back online. Critical for low-connectivity regions.

FREE 🌍 Global Impact

🟡 TIER 2 — Advanced Features (Free + Pro)

🤖

AI Word Prediction

On-device ML predicts next likely symbol as user communicates. Learns from individual usage patterns. Reduces taps by up to 60%. Basic (free) vs. self-learning engine (Pro).

FREE (Basic) PRO (Advanced)
👤

Multi-User Profiles

Each patient/family member gets their own vocabulary, grid size, and preferences. Caregiver admin profile manages all users. Free: 2 profiles. Pro: unlimited.

FREE (2) PRO (Unlimited)
🌐

Family & Therapist Portal

Browser-based dashboard for parents and speech-language pathologists. Edit boards remotely, view usage reports, share vocabulary sets. Free: read-only. Pro: full editing.

FREE (Read) PRO (Full Edit)
📊

Progress Tracking

Track word usage frequency, daily session count, communication complexity over time. Visual charts. Exportable IEP reports for schools. Milestone celebrations to motivate children.

PRO Feature

FREE

🟢 TIER 3 — AI-Powered Differentiators (Unique to Fabulaa)

🧠

AI Communication Assistant

User taps [I] + [want] + [pizza] → AI expands to "I would like to have pizza, please." Powered by Gemini API. Context-aware suggestions. No competitor offers this today.

PRO ⭐ Unique Feature
🎮

Gamified Learning Mode

Interactive games teaching AAC vocabulary. Match symbols to pictures, build sentences, earn badges and streaks. Designed for children (3+), adaptable for adults. SLP-assignable goals.

FREE ⭐ Unique Feature
📖

Visual Story Builder

Create visual social stories — step-by-step picture sequences for doctor visits, daily routines, social skills. Record narration. Library of pre-made stories. Share with family/therapists.

FREE

Users at risk of losing their voice (ALS, cancer) record their voice while still able. App creates a personalised TTS voice that sounds exactly like them. Standalone apps charge $500+ for this.

Always-accessible pre-loaded board: body pain diagram, pain scale 1-10, emergency phrases ("Call 911", "I am allergic to…"), full offline, accessible from lock screen shortcut.

FREE ⭐ Life-Saving

Smart Home Integration

Control Google Home / Amazon Alexa devices through the AAC board. Control lights, TV, thermostat with symbol taps. Tobii Dynavox charges $3,000+ hardware for this capability.

PRO ⭐ Unique Feature

Dual-screen mode: AAC user mode + conversation partner mode. Partner types responses that display visually. Phrase suggestions for partners. Makes natural two-way conversation easy.

Next Steps
Open Questions

To finalise the development plan and begin execution, we need your input on the following key decisions.

1

Primary Target User — Children with autism? Adults with ALS or stroke? Hearing impaired users? Each group has different UI needs and will influence the design direction.

2

Priority Regions & Markets — Which countries or regions should we prioritise? This determines which languages get built first and which symbol cultural contexts matter most.

3

Development Approach — Build internally with a dedicated team, or work with an outsourced development partner? This affects the timeline and tech stack choice.

4

Existing User Migration — Is there an existing user database from the current Fabulaa app that needs to be migrated to the new platform?

5

Budget for Phase 1 — What is the available development budget for the first 4 months? This determines the size and structure of the initial development team.

6

Brand Decision — Should the current app be updated gradually, or is this a brand-new release under "Fabulaa 2.0" with a fresh launch strategy?

7

Monetisation Priority — Is the primary goal revenue sustainability (Pro subscribers first) or maximum impact (free user growth first)?

8

Clinical Advisors — Are there Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs) or occupational therapists available to consult during the design and testing phases?

9

Symbol Library License — ARASAAC is free for non-commercial use. A commercial license may be needed. Is the client open to this arrangement?
